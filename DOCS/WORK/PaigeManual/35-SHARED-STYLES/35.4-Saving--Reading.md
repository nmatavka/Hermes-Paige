## 35.4 Saving & Reading

Saving the individual shared `pg_ref`s or controls works the same as before: when you call `pgSaveDoc()` or `pgCacheSaveDoc()`, HERMES Paige realises that some of the} structures are shared with a master `pg_ref`, and therefore those structures are not saved to the disk file. Hence, you eliminate excess file overhead. This is also true for saving a control with `PG_SAVEFILE` or `PG_CACHESAVEFILE`, as well as saving with the HERMES Paige Export extension to "native" format.

Reading a shared `pg_ref` or control also works as before (`pgReadDoc()`, `pgCacheReadDoc()`, or `PG_READFILE` and `PG_CACHEREADFILE`). However, you must first create an empty "shared" `pg_ref` or control before reading the file.
