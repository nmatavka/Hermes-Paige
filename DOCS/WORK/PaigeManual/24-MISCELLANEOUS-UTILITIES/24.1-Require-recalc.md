## 24.1 Require `recalc`

	(void) pgInvalSelect (pg_ref pg, long select_from, long select_to);

The text from `select_from` to `select_to` in `pg` is *invalidated*, i.e., marked to require recalculation, new word wrap, etc.

Both parameters are zero-indexed byte offsets. No actual calculation is performed until `pgPaginateNow` is called (see section 24.3, [Paginate Now](#24.3-paginate-now)) or the text (or highlighting) is drawn.
